XP League × Newegg Gamer Zone

We've partnered with XP League to deliver weekly Fortnite trainings and tournaments for all ages. Every Wednesday and Friday, XP League coaches, students, and players train interested youth to play Fortnite, VALORANT, and other games at a professional level — new keybinds, strategies, and ways to play.

Wednesdays — training

Coached sessions: mechanics, builds, game sense. Check the calendar for this week's flyer.

Fridays — tournament

Put the training to work in bracket play. End-of-month editions add a party and prizes.
