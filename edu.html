XP League × Newegg Gamer Zone

We've partnered with XP League to deliver biweekly Fortnite trainings and tournaments for all ages. On the first and third week of each month, XP League coaches, students, and players train interested youth to play Fortnite, VALORANT, and other games at a professional level — new keybinds, strategies, and ways to play, with a Wednesday training session and a Friday tournament each of those weeks.

Wednesdays — training

1st & 3rd week of the month. Coached sessions: mechanics, builds, game sense. Check the calendar for this week's flyer.

Fridays — tournament

1st & 3rd week of the month. Put the training to work in bracket play. End-of-month editions add a party and prizes.
