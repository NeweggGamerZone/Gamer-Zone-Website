About Gamer Zone

A 2,300 sq. ft. gaming and content-creation space inside Newegg HQ in Diamond Bar — built for free play, esports, streaming, and hands-on tech education. Here's what's inside — auto-cycles every minute, or flip through it yourself.

ZONE 1

PC Gaming Zone

25+ prebuilt gaming PCs with Intel and AMD Ryzen processors, NVIDIA GeForce RTX and AMD Radeon graphics, and pro-grade peripherals, webcams, and Stream Decks — all set up with CORSAIR gaming chairs and COUGAR desks.

ZONE 2

Racing & Immersive Zone

Triple-monitor racing rigs with force-feedback wheels and pedals from Fanatec, MOZA, and Thermaltake, plus a semi-enclosed Cooler Master ORB X pod for a fully wrapped-in driving experience.

ZONE 3

VR & Mixed Reality Zone

Meta Quest VR paired with the Virtuix Omni One treadmill for full 360° motion tracking — walk, crouch, and jump right in the game. A favorite for first-timers and livestream demos alike.

ZONE 4

Console Gaming Zone

Xbox, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, and a retro games emulator, ready for couch co-op, classic showcases, and casual multiplayer nights.

ZONE 5

Broadcast

A dedicated setup for livestreaming, tournaments, and shoutcasting — built to bring the action to viewers watching from home.
