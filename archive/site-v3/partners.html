How it works

The event application asks for your organizer info, group type (school, corporate, or media), guest count and age group, event purpose, and any catering needs (food, drinks, and more can be arranged). Our team responds within ~10 business days, then we build the run-of-show together — layout, hardware, staffing, catering, marketing plan — through event day and a recap with photos, Bulletin posts, and community metrics.
