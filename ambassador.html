Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Community leaders, esports teams, and event organizers: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone. Bring a crowd, build your community in our room — and unlock more with every event you host as an Ambassador.

Apply to become an Ambassador

How it works

With a minimum requirement of 20 attendees, we're inviting visitors to host their communities at the Newegg Gamer Zone! Apply below — tell us your event type, how many events you can host, and your expected attendance. Host at your own pace and climb the tiers as you go — hit bigger numbers at any single event and stack an attendance bonus on top. Questions? Ask us on Discord, Instagram, or Email.

Who it's for

Community Leaders

Community organizers and content creators who rally gamers and fans for meetups and LAN sessions.

Esports Teams

Collegiate teams, clubs and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments.

Event Organizers

Creators, Vendors, and Local Businesses who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions.
