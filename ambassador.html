Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Enthusiasts, esports members, and event hosts: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone. Bring a crowd, build your community in our room — and unlock more with every event you host across a 6-month Ambassador journey.

Apply to become an Ambassador See the 6-month journey

Who it's for

Enthusiasts

Community organizers and creators who can rally players for regular meetups and game nights.

Esports members

Teams, clubs, and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments.

Event hosts

Creators, brands, and organizers who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions.

The one requirement: just keep hosting. There's no attendee minimum to hit — tiers unlock based on how many events you've hosted, not how many people show up. Bring a bigger crowd, though, and you'll unlock attendance bonuses on top.

Your 6-month Ambassador journey

Tiers unlock based on cumulative events hosted — no attendee minimums, no monthly deadlines. Host at your own pace over your first 6 months and climb.

How it works: host events at your own pace — hit the cumulative total to unlock a tier and roll into the next one. Tiers are cumulative: once you unlock a tier, you keep every benefit from the tiers below it as you climb.
