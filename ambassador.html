Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Enthusiasts, esports members, and event hosts: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone. Bring a crowd, build your community in our room — and unlock more each month across a one-year Ambassador journey.

Apply to become an Ambassador See the 12-month journey

Who it's for

Enthusiasts

Community organizers and creators who can rally players for regular meetups and game nights.

Esports members

Teams, clubs, and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments.

Event hosts

Creators, brands, and organizers who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions.

The one requirement: each event needs to hit an agreed attendee quota so the room stays lively and worth hosting. We'll set your quota together based on your event type when you apply — then it's your room to run.

Your 12-month Ambassador journey

A one-year commitment, one milestone at a time. Each month raises the bar on attendance a little — and unlocks the next benefit. Targets below are starting points; we tailor them to your event type when you apply.

1
Month 1 · Kickoff
15+ attendees / event
Welcome aboard

Free use of the Zone, priority date booking, a dedicated GZ contact, and your events listed on our calendar & socials.

2
Month 2
20+ attendees / event
Branded setup

On-site signage and hands-on setup help from GZ staff for every event.

3
Month 3

We cover refreshments for your attendees, plus a featured promo push across GZ channels.

8

40+ attendees / event
Catered meals

Catered food for your events, on us.

10
Month 10
45+ attendees / event
Co-branded promotion

Co-branded marketing and a hardware/vendor sponsorship for a flagship event.

12
Month 12 · Full Ambassador
50+ attendees / event
Renew as a Senior Ambassador

First look at new GZ tech, a co-branded major event, and a renewed year with your milestones carried forward.

How it works: hit that month's attendee target to unlock and keep the benefit and roll into the next milestone. Miss a month and you simply hold your current perks until you hit the target again — no penalty, just keep hosting.
