Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Enthusiasts, esports members, and event hosts: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone. Bring a crowd, build your community in our room — and unlock bigger perks the longer you host with us.

Apply to become an Ambassador See the perks

Who it's for

Enthusiasts

Community organizers and creators who can rally players for regular meetups and game nights.

Esports members

Teams, clubs, and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments.

Event hosts

Creators, brands, and organizers who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions.

The one requirement: each event needs to hit an agreed attendee quota so the room stays lively and worth hosting. We'll set your quota together based on your event type when you apply — then it's your room to run.
