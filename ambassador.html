Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Collegiate clubs, individual creators, and organizations: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone for FREE. Bring a crowd and build your community using our venue and unlock more with every event you host as an Ambassador.

Apply to become an Ambassador

How it works

With a minimum requirement of 20 attendees, we're inviting visitors to host their communities at the Newegg Gamer Zone! Apply below: pick your track, tell us your event type, how many events you can host, and your expected attendance. Host at your own pace and climb the tiers as you go, hitting bigger numbers at any single event to stack an attendance bonus on top. Influencer and Organization events happen here at the Zone; Collegiate can host here too (with a more thorough application) or bring Newegg out to a sponsored event on your own campus. Questions? Ask us on Discord, Instagram, or Email.

Ambassador events at the Zone
