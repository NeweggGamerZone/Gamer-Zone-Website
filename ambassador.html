Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Community leaders, esports teams, and event organizers: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone. Bring a crowd, build your community in our room — and unlock more with every event you host as an Ambassador.

Apply to become an Ambassador

Who it's for

Community Leaders

Community organizers and creators who can rally players for regular meetups and game nights.

Esports Teams

Teams, clubs, and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments.

Event Organizers

Creators, brands, and hosts who run showcases, watch parties, and meetups.

The one requirement: Minimum of 20 attendees per event to qualify. From there, Ambassadors progress on two independent tracks.

Track 1 — Number of Events

Your tier (Welcome Ambassador through Senior Ambassador) unlocks based on how many events you've cumulatively hosted — see Your Ambassador Journey below.

Track 2 — Number of Attendees

Bring a bigger crowd to any single event and stack an attendance bonus on top — independent of your tier or timeline.

Your Ambassador Journey

Tiers unlock based on cumulative events hosted — no deadlines, no clock running. Host at your own pace and climb.
