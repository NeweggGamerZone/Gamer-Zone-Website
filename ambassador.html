Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Community leaders, esports teams, and influencers: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone for FREE. Bring a crowd and build your community using our venue and unlock more with every event you host as an Ambassador.

Apply to become an Ambassador

How it works

With a minimum requirement of 20 attendees, we're inviting visitors to host their communities at the Newegg Gamer Zone! Apply below — tell us your event type, how many events you can host, and your expected attendance. Host at your own pace and climb the tiers as you go — hit bigger numbers at any single event and stack an attendance bonus on top. Questions? Ask us on Discord, Instagram, or Email.

Choose Your Class

Every Ambassador brings something different to the Zone — pick the archetype that matches how your community plays.

Class: Shield

Community Leaders

Community organizers and content creators who rally gamers and fans for meetups and LAN sessions — the ones who hold the group together.

Class: Sword

Esports Teams

Collegiate teams, clubs and leagues that want a home venue for scrims, tryouts, and tournaments — built for head-on competition.

Class: Bow

Event Organizers

Creators, Vendors, and Local Businesses who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions — planning the whole encounter from a distance.
