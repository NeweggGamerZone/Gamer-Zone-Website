Gamer Zone Ambassadors

Collegiate clubs, individual creators, and organizations: run your events at the Newegg Gamer Zone for FREE. Bring a crowd and build your community using our venue and unlock more with every event you host as an Ambassador.

Apply to become an Ambassador

How it works

Bring your community to host at the Newegg Gamer Zone, free — here's the short version:

  • Apply below (min. 20 attendees) and host at your own pace, climbing tiers and stacking attendance bonuses as you go.
  • Influencer and Organization events happen here at the Zone; Collegiate can host here too, or bring a sponsored event to your own campus.

Questions? Ask us on Discord, Instagram, or Email.

Ambassador events at the Zone

A few of the real events Ambassadors have run here: USC Games at Evil Geniuses' VALORANT Collegiate Cup, and a UGREEN x WD hands-on tech session.

Choose Your Track

Every Ambassador brings something different to the Zone: pick the track that matches how you host.

Track: Collegiate

Collegiate

School clubs and officers hosting on-campus brand representation, school events, and content. Can host here at the Zone (a deeper application, including a club sponsorship agreement) or bring a sponsored Newegg event to your own campus.

Apply as a Collegiate Ambassador
Track: Influencer

Influencer

Individual creators who bring their own community to the Zone for meetups, content sessions, and LAN nights — built around your audience, hosted here.

Apply as an Influencer Ambassador
Track: Organization

Organization

Clubs, vendors, and local businesses — including small shops like TCG, game, and tech stores — who run showcases, watch parties, and competitions here at the Zone.

Apply as an Organization Ambassador
