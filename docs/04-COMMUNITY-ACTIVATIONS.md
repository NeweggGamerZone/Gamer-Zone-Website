# Community Activations — In-Room, Local, and Global

Goal: make GZ a community you can belong to from anywhere, with the room as its beating heart. Every activation has an **in-person lane** and a **remote lane**, and most feed the Wall (the visible proof of community that also sells vendors).

---

## 1. The Wall as the engine

The Graffiti Wall is not a feature; it's the flywheel:

```
visit → sign the wall → post published → friends see it online
  → they visit / submit remotely → wall gets livelier
  → liveliness attracts new visitors AND vendor partners → more events
  → more reasons to visit → repeat
```

Post types seed the culture: 📝 notes/signatures · 😂 GZ memes · 🏆 prize brags ("won the ROG mousepad!") · 🎮 LFG ("looking for sim racing rivals, Saturdays") · 🔧 tech tips. Remote posts get a 🌍 badge — locals see the world watching; remotes see they count.

## 2. Recurring activations (low lift, run forever)

| Activation | In-person lane | Remote lane | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sign the Wall** | Kiosk tablet at exit: "leave your mark" | Discord #gz-wall submissions | Always on |
| **Meme of the Month** | Printed top-3 on the physical wall | Submit + vote in Discord; winner featured on Home | Monthly |
| **Prize Cabinet** | Photo station when someone wins a giveaway | Winners post pics from home w/ prize | Per event |
| **Global Setup Showdown** | GZ's own battlestations as the benchmark | Post your home setup; community picks a champ | Quarterly |
| **Theme Night Companion** | The night itself | Same game/challenge playable at home that week ("GZ Time Trial: beat our sim lap time, post your screenshot") | Weekly |
| **LFG Board** | Meet at theme nights | Wall LFG posts + Discord matchmaking channel | Always on |
| **Tech Corner** | Ask staff at the bench | Discord Q&A; best answers become wall 🔧 posts | Always on |

## 3. Signature ideas (bigger swings)

- **GZ Passport (loyalty placeholder → program):** stamp card (physical + digital later). Visits, theme nights, wall posts, tournament entries earn stamps → tiers (Bronze Egg → Golden Egg). Referral = bring a first-timer, both get a stamp. *Site already reserves the Rewards section for this.*
- **World Wall Map:** a map graphic on the Community page pinning where remote wall-signers are from — "GZ has been signed from 23 countries."
- **Lap Time Ladder:** persistent sim-racing leaderboard (photo of the in-room board posted biweekly; remote ghosts via matching game/track at home).
- **Vendor community showcases:** vendors don't just demo — they sponsor a wall month, a meme contest, or the Passport tier prizes. This is the story the Partners page sells: *activate an engaged community, not a room rental.*
- **EDU artifacts:** each school workshop ends with the class signing the wall — parents look it up at home. Field trip → family visit funnel.

## 4. Discord strategy

Recommendation: don't fork a separate server yet. Use the existing discord.gg/newegg with dedicated channels (#gamer-zone, #gz-wall, #gz-lfg, #gz-tech-corner) — instant audience, zero moderation burden to bootstrap. Revisit a standalone server when GZ channels outgrow the host. (Open question #3 in PRD.)

## 5. Measurement

Wall posts/week (split local vs 🌍 remote), meme contest entries, Discord channel growth, Passport signups (later), % of theme-night attendees who found it via site. Screenshot monthly for the vendor deck — community metrics are the Partners-page sales asset.
