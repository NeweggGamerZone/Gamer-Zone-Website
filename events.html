How Registration Works

Preregistering isn't required, walk-ins are always welcome, but it's the fastest way to start gaming at the Newegg Gamer Zone! Here's how it works, start to finish.

1

Preregister online

Tap the Preregister button and fill out our security check and waiver. Once you register, you'll get a Verkada guest pass in your email inbox for fast check-ins on future visits.

2

Check in at the door

When you arrive, scan your preregistration or guest pass or, if you didn't preregister, just sign in at the front desk.

3

Register a SENET ID

After checking in, you'll register a SENET ID at the desk, which is what logs you into our gaming PC. Username and age details can be adjusted at SENET.ID

Fastest way to check in: preregister once and your Verkada guest pass lands in your email — just scan it at the door on every future visit, no re-registering needed.
