How preregistering works

Preregistering isn't required — walk-ins are always welcome — but it's the fastest way in. Here's how it works, start to finish.

1. Preregister online

Tap the Preregister button and fill out the quick form. Once you register, you'll get a guest pass on file for quicker check-ins on future visits.

2. Check in at the door

When you arrive, show your preregistration — or, if you didn't preregister, just sign in at the front desk.

3. Register a SENET ID

After checking in, you'll register a SENET ID at the desk — that's what logs you into our gaming PCs and consoles.
